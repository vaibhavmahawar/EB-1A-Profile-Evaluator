Synthetic Candidate: Elena Torres

Elena Torres is a professional violinist and composer. Her original composition was performed at the National Performing Arts Exhibition in 2021 and 2022. Her album sold 85,000 paid downloads and reached number three on a national classical music chart. She received the International Young Composer Prize. A feature about her work appeared in Music Review Weekly, a national publication. She has performed as principal violinist with the Metropolitan Chamber Orchestra, a distinguished organization.
